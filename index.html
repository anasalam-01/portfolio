CURRENT PROJECT

TRIREN

A student opportunity discovery platform that brings internships, hackathons, scholarships, competitions, research programs, and career opportunities together in one place.

Students—especially from Tier 2/3/4 colleges—often miss valuable opportunities because information is scattered across LinkedIn, college groups, websites, WhatsApp communities, and individual organizations.

Triren brings internships, hackathons, scholarships, competitions, research programs, fellowships, courses, and career opportunities together in one place.

RFID Door Locking System

Developed an RFID-based door locking system using Arduino. Authorized RFID cards/tags are verified before unlocking, while a servo motor controls the lock and an I2C LCD displays access status.
